Our story

A letter from our founder

Every child is born a scientist, an artist, a builder and a philosopher, all at once. Somewhere between the first day of school and the last, most of us are quietly taught to be none of those things.

Delta World School in Hapur is being built because we refuse to accept that this is how education has to be. It is the classroom we all wish we had grown up in.

“Curious, confident, kind, skilled and rooted: whole human beings, ready for life.”

With an experience of more than thirty years in Indian education, and three generations of serving children’s educational development, we have carried that trust forward, and built something new with it through this school.

Our approach
